# Dataset sources and notes

## Venue and location references

- **Dim Tu Tac**, 55 Đông Du: venue name, address, and photo coordinates are stated on [the Commons venue photo page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dim_Tu_Tac_flagship_location,_55_%C4%90%C3%B4ng_Du_Street.jpg). The image license is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Ốc La Cà**, 248A Đồng Đen: [Riviu location listing](https://riviu.vn/quan-oc-la-ca-dong-den). The paired Commons files are photos named for Ốc La Cà.
- **Ốc Hai Lùn**, Lê Đại Hành: [venue reference](https://ghiensaigon.com/quan-oc-hai-lun/). The Commons photo set names the restaurant and street; the exact pin is approximate.
- **Hum Lounge**, 34 Võ Văn Tần: the Commons image identifies Hum Lounge; the current address is confirmed by [the official Hum locations page](https://hum-dining.vn/en/locations/). The paired menu photo is a representative vegetarian dish photographed in Ho Chi Minh City and is not confirmed as a current Hum menu item.
- **Cơm Tấm Cali**, 235 Hoàng Diệu: [Q4 venue reference](https://guidebold.com/guide/com-tam-cali-hoang-dieu-quan-4-tp-hcm/). Commons includes a Q4 interior photo and a dish photo captioned as taken at that branch.
- **Bánh Xèo 46A**, 46A Đinh Công Tráng: [official venue page](https://www.bwhouse.vn/banhxeo46a). The Commons venue photo shows the 46A sign; the paired menu photo is a representative bánh xèo image.
- **Bún Bò 1980**, Tân Phú: the venue name and neighborhood come from the Commons photo title. House number and current operating status need confirmation.
- **Chiang Rai Thai Restaurant**, 710/5 Lũy Bán Bích: [location reference](https://localvn.whereplace.com/hcm/?food=chiang+rai+-+thai+restaurant). The Commons photo shows the restaurant sign.
- **Anan Saigon**, 89 Tôn Thất Đạm: [official restaurant site](https://anansaigon.com/) and [Commons venue photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anan_Saigon.jpg).
- **Maison Marou**, District 1: the [Commons photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maison_Marou_Ho_Chi_Minh_City.jpg) identifies the store and provides a camera location; the exact branch address should be reconfirmed.
- **Café City Garden**, Tân Bình: venue name and area come from the Commons photo set; the address and map pin are approximate.
- **Café 30/4**, 135 Nam Kỳ Khởi Nghĩa: venue, address, and coordinates are stated on [the Commons photo page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2023-12-10_Caf%C3%A9_30%EF%BC%8F4,_Nam_K%E1%BB%B3_Kh%E1%BB%9Fi_Ngh%C4%A9a_Street,_Ho_Chi_Minh_City.jpg).

## Photo licensing

All 24 files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at up to 1280 px. The per-file source page, author, license URL, attribution, and whether a menu photo is verified at the named venue are recorded in `image-rights.csv`. Preserve those attributions when using the assets. Photo license metadata is taken from each Commons file page through the Commons API.

## Coordinates and routes

Coordinates are demo pins. For photo files with geotags, the point may be the photographer's camera position rather than a confirmed entrance; other points are approximate. Tour ordering is an unverified demo itinerary. Obtain route geometry from the routing service and confirm the stops/transport mode before public release.
